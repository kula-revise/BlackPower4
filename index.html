Remember this

The Panthers brought people together most through the breakfasts and clinics, not the rifles. The guns gave the police and the FBI a reason to attack, and that broke the party. The programmes did the real organising, more quietly.

Judge the party by everything it did, not just the guns that got the most attention. It is one of the points you will use when you build an essay.
