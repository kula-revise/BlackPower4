The essay so far

Four moves. Read the whole thing through: this is now a line of argument, not a set of notes.

Black Power grew out of conditions that the Civil Rights Movement's legal victories never reached, so its militancy began as an answer to continued poverty and police brutality. At its core Black Power was a philosophy of self-definition and self-reliance, so its militancy has to be judged as one expression of that philosophy and not as its purpose. Malcolm X gave the militant strand its clearest voice by defending armed self-defence, yet the same argument insisted on self-respect and self-help. The Black Panther Party carried both faces at once, patrolling the police while running feeding, literacy and childcare schemes, which shows militancy and self-empowerment coming from a single idea.

Four sentences. That is a full line of argument, and you built it one decision at a time.
